Privacy

Your scores stay on this device

Fifer is a static quiz. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not send your answers to a Fifer server.

What is stored

Your streak, scores, and an in-progress attempt are saved in this browser with localStorage (key fifer.v1). That data stays on your device. Clearing site data for this site, or using another browser or device, starts you fresh. Fifer cannot see or recover it.

What we do not collect

There is no sign-in, no profile, and no Fifer account. The quiz and the records page load public files (the question bank and the records tables) from this site. Sharing a result opens X only if you choose Share on X or follow a link; that is handled by X, not by Fifer.

Advertising and cookies

The quiz is free. Ad spaces on the pages are placeholders today and do not load an ad network. If advertising is added later (for example Google AdSense), those providers may set cookies or similar technologies to measure and show ads. This page will be updated if that happens. You can control cookies in your browser settings.
